==================================================
2. WHY THE DIALOGUE PLATFORM WAS CREATED
==================================================

The Dialogue Platform was established around a fundamental problem:

Sudanese society has experienced repeated wars, political polarization, mistrust, fragmentation and cycles of violence.

Instead of creating another space where participants simply defend predetermined political positions, The Dialogue Platform was conceived as a place where people can:

- meet across differences;
- listen to one another;
- discuss difficult issues respectfully;
- understand competing perspectives;
- rebuild trust;
- challenge polarization;
- explore peaceful solutions;
- strengthen a culture of dialogue;
- and develop shared visions for the future.

One of the recurring questions underlying the platform's work is:

"Why do wars and conflicts keep recurring in Sudan, and how can trust and sustainable peace be built?"

The platform is based on the conviction that sustainable peace cannot be achieved only through political agreements. Trust between people, communities and institutions must also be rebuilt.

Two ideas strongly represent its philosophy:

"Peace begins with a word."

and:

"Building trust requires dialogue."

==================================================
3. MISSION
==================================================

The Dialogue Platform promotes long-lasting peace, sustainable trust and peaceful conflict resolution by creating inclusive and independent spaces where people with different backgrounds, experiences and perspectives can meet, listen, reflect and engage in meaningful dialogue.

The platform seeks to transform disagreement from a source of division into an opportunity for understanding.

Its broader objective is to contribute to societies where differences can be managed peacefully rather than through violence, exclusion or dehumanization.

4. CORE VALUES
==================================================

The Dialogue Platform is guided by:

INCLUSION
Different voices and perspectives should have access to dialogue.

OPENNESS
Participants should be able to discuss difficult questions honestly.

RESPECT
Disagreement should never eliminate human dignity.

INDEPENDENCE
The platform should remain a space for dialogue rather than becoming an instrument of a political faction.

COOPERATION
Peacebuilding requires cooperation across communities, institutions and sectors.

TRUST
Trust is treated as a prerequisite for sustainable peace.

NON-VIOLENCE
Political and social differences should be addressed through peaceful means.

HUMAN DIGNITY
People must remain at the center of dialogue regardless of identity or political position.

5. DIALOGUE METHODOLOGY
==================================================

The Dialogue Platform's methodology has been influenced by dialogue facilitation approaches associated with the Nansen Center for Peace and Dialogue.

The work emphasizes:

- active listening;
- open-ended questions;
- exploration rather than debate;
- understanding rather than defeating an opponent;
- reflection;
- exchange of experiences;
- recognition of differences;
- trust-building;
- safe and respectful dialogue spaces;
- and continued engagement rather than one-off confrontation.

The platform does not require participants to agree.

Its purpose is to make meaningful communication possible even when agreement does not exist.

Omran Adam completed Nansen training in the Public Dialogue Approach in Lillehammer, 6–10 October 2025, strengthening the methodological foundation of the platform's dialogue work.

The event demonstrated several principles that would become central to the platform:

- community dialogue;
- accessibility;
- Sudanese diaspora engagement;
- peacebuilding;
- inclusion;
- food and social interaction;
- and provision for participants with children.

From these early activities, the platform developed into a regular dialogue initiative.

38. POLITICAL INDEPENDENCE
==================================================

This point is critical for all website content.

The Dialogue Platform engages people with different political perspectives.

People may disagree strongly about Sudan's past, present and future while still sharing the responsibility to communicate peacefully.

46. CORE WEBSITE NARRATIVE
==================================================

The Dialogue Platform was created from a simple but difficult idea:

People do not have to agree in order to speak to one another.

Against the background of war, displacement and increasing polarization in Sudan, The Dialogue Platform began building spaces where people with different experiences and perspectives could meet, listen and engage without being required to abandon their differences.

Beginning with community dialogue in Lillestrøm, Norway, the initiative developed recurring dialogue sessions, partnerships, digital programming and community engagement.

Its work subsequently expanded toward refugee-led peacebuilding and social-cohesion initiatives in Uganda.

Today, the platform is developing a model that connects community dialogue, peacebuilding, youth engagement, media, humanitarian solidarity and international cooperation.

The goal is not to manufacture agreement.

The goal is to rebuild the human relationships that make peaceful disagreement, trust and ultimately sustainable peace possible.

47. SHORT ORGANIZATIONAL DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Dialogue Platform (المنتدى الحواري) is an independent dialogue and peacebuilding initiative based in Lillestrøm, Norway. It creates inclusive spaces where people with different backgrounds and perspectives can meet, listen and engage in meaningful dialogue. Emerging from the Sudanese diaspora experience, the platform works to build trust, reduce polarization, promote peaceful conflict resolution and strengthen coexistence through community dialogue, media, youth engagement and refugee-led initiatives in Norway and Uganda.
